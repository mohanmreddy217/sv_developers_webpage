About SV Developers

Building Trust. Creating Better Living Spaces.

SV Developers is a real estate development company focused on creating quality residential plots, farm villa plots, premium villa plots, and thoughtfully designed apartments across Aler, Bhongir, and Shamirpet in the Hyderabad region.

We believe that finding the right property is more than just an investment — it is a step toward building a secure future. With a focus on transparent dealings, well-planned layouts, and convenient locations, we aim to make property ownership a smooth and rewarding experience for every customer.

Our projects are designed to offer a combination of accessibility, essential infrastructure, and long-term value, helping customers make confident decisions about their residential and investment needs.

Our Vision

To become a trusted name in real estate by developing quality properties that combine thoughtful planning, transparency, and lasting value for homeowners and investors.

Our Mission

  • Transparency and Trust: Promote clear property documentation and transparent dealings.
  • Quality Development: Focus on well-planned layouts, infrastructure, and comfortable living environments.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Help customers make informed property decisions with reliable guidance and site visits.
  • Strategic Locations: Develop residential projects in promising locations around Hyderabad, including Aler, Bhongir, and Shamirpet.
  • Long-Term Value: Offer property options suited to both residential aspirations and investment goals.

About SV Apartment

SV Apartment is an HMDA approved residential apartment project near Shamirpet offering well-designed 2 BHK deluxe flats of 945 SFT with 100% Vaasthu, clear title, spot registration, security, CCTV surveillance and four-side roads.

  • 2 BHK deluxe flats of 945 SFT with 100% Vaasthu compliance, four-side roads and full ventilation
  • HMDA approved, with clear title and spot registration
  • 24-hour security, CCTV surveillance, entrance and exit gates, car parking, generator, transformer, hi-phase power connection and sewerage
  • Earthquake-resistant RCC framed structure, 9-inch external walls, 4-inch internal walls and light-weight red bricks
  • Granite or marble flooring in common areas, UPVC windows with safety grills, teak wood door frames and designer internal shutters
  • Kitchen provisions: stainless steel sink, chimney, exhaust fan and water purifier
  • Bathrooms with white EWC, wash basins, health faucets, hot and cold water mixers, geyser provisions, glazed ceramic tile dado and anti-skid flooring
  • Fire-resistant concealed copper wiring, with outlets for ACs and geysers and provisions for washing machine, water purifier, chimney, refrigerator, microwave oven and mixer
  • 6 passenger V3F lifts, overhead water tank, bore water and drinking water
